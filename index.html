      About and safety

      This is a self-directed practice tool. It is not medical advice and it does not diagnose anything. If hoarseness, pain or voice changes last more than two weeks, see an ENT doctor or a speech-language pathologist.

      Your voice is analysed on this device as you speak. No audio is stored or sent anywhere; only measurements are kept.

      Rewards are for practising, for using the techniques, and for taking on harder speaking situations. Stuttering during practice never costs you anything.
